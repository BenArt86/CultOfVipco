Get Involved

The Cult of Vipco has grown through the support of collectors worldwide. We have expanded the archive to state now that the book is looking highly likely to happen! However, there is still a long way to go, and you might have scans that we need.

If you see a sleeve on the website that has ‘Temp Art’ written on it, or if any scan you see looks lesser in condition compared to a scan you have, we would love to receive a 300dpi scan of your sleeve for the book. Contact us today to discuss sending scans.

In return, everyone who contributes at this stage will have a dedicated thank you in the final book. You can join the supporters who have helped us to get this far over at the supporters page.

How do I send you a scan of my sleeve?

D.I.Y Method
Set your scanner to 300DPI (lower DPI won’t be suitable for glossy book printing).
Preview the scan to ensure nothing is cropped (Epson scanners often crop edges).
Apply gentle pressure to the scanner lid (we use three VHS tapes) to flatten the spine area.
Contact us via the Contact page for the dedicated email address for receiving scans.

W.D.I (We Do It) Method
We can arrange for you to post us your sleeves via tracked/signed-for first class Royal Mail. We will return them the same way, and we cover the cost. This method works, but it’s preferable for us if you keep your sleeves safe at home.

Can I send multiple scans at once?

Yes! WeTransfer is free for up to 2GB and handles high resolution files easily. Once your scans are ready, contact us and we’ll send you the email address to upload them to.

Do you buy films/sleeves?

If it has “VIPCO” on it and we don’t have it, we’ll do our best to meet your price. Get in touch and let’s see what you’ve got.

See a missing title?

Vipco’s catalogue is full of surprises. If you spot a title we haven’t listed, or know its VIP number, your info helps complete the archive. Even better if you have a scan!

See a mistake?

If you notice an incorrect date, title, or release detail, let us know. Accuracy matters, and your knowledge helps keep the archive historically correct.

Want your name removed from the Thanks Page?

No problem, just message us. If you prefer privacy, your name will be respectfully omitted from both the site and the book.

I have more questions, how do I get in touch?

For any other questions, visit the Contact page and drop us a message. We look forward to hearing from you.
