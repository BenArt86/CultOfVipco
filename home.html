Welcome to the Cult of Vipco

Cult of Vipco is a collector led archive built from years of hunting, scanning, restoring, and cataloguing every known Vipco VHS and DVD sleeve, from Introducing Badminton to The Claw, and everything in between.

What began as a personal preservation project has grown into a full scale effort to document Vipco’s entire visual history, forming the foundation of a forthcoming full gloss coffee table art book celebrating the bold, chaotic world of an infamous British home video horror label.

The archive continues to expand through community support. If you have scans, missing titles, or knowledge that can help complete the collection, your contribution is welcome. Explore the collection, discover the oddities, and help preserve Vipco’s legacy.
