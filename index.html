Welcome to The Cult of Vipco, a visual collector’s guide to every known release from Britain’s most notorious horror home video label. This project documents the artwork that defined Vipco’s output, while also exploring the censorship history, cultural impact, and collector mythology surrounding the label’s VHS and DVD releases.
